## Vocational scenario or context (verbatim so far)

You have just started work as a **junior systems analyst** in the project department at **ViraMedia**, a marketing and design company.

You have been asked to work in a project team to **develop a new e-card system for Say-It-With-Cards**.

**Say-It-With-Cards** currently offer a small range of online designer greetings cards. The current system **does not have a facility for customers to personalise the cards**.

The new e-card system will offer customers **various animated designer cards** such as:
- Birthday
- Valentine's Day
- Thank you
- Christmas

Customers will be given the facility to:
- **Personalise cards**
- **Upload images**
- **Upload to social media**

### The system should allow the customer to:

- Create a **diary** to record events such as birthdays.
- Access a **quick view of events and reminders**.
- Access a **quick view address book**.
- Send **automatic reminders** of the diary events, **3 days before the event**.
- **Link to preferred social media**.
- **View on both mobile devices and desktop PC**.
- **Send e-cards from the desktop**.
- **Receive card receipt confirmation**.
- **Access payment facilities**.
- **Have a secure login**.

## What we know so far — extracted requirements checklist

### Functional requirements (from the visible bullets)

- [ ] **Card catalogue** — animated designer cards categorised by occasion (Birthday, Valentine's Day, Thank you, Christmas).

- [ ] **Image upload** — customer uploads their own images into cards.
- [ ] **Social media upload / share** — send cards to preferred social media.
- [ ] **Personal diary** — customer stores events (e.g. contacts' birthdays).
- [ ] **Quick view of events + reminders** — dashboard.
- [ ] **Quick view address book** — customer's contacts / recipients.
- [ ] **Automatic reminders** — sent **3 days before** each diary event.
- [ ] **Link to preferred social media** — account linking.

- [ ] **Receive card receipt confirmation** — confirmation to sender that a card was delivered/opened.
- [ ] **Payment facilities** — customer can pay for cards (implies pricing, checkout, payment provider integration).
- [ ] **Secure login** — user account with authentication (implies user registration, password/2FA, session management).
