## Mark Scheme — Assessment Objectives (AOs)

Unit 3 is graded by Assessment Objectives, not P/M/D criteria. Every answer in the exam should hit the AO the question is asking for. To aim for **Distinction**, answers must reach **AO3 and AO4** — not just describe.

| AO  | What it tests                                                            | Command words to look for                                                                | How to write for it                                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AO1 | **Knowledge and understanding** of project planning & management         | identify, describe, state, outline, define                                               | Give correct terms and clear definitions. Short factual points.                           |
| AO2 | **Apply** knowledge to the given project scenario                        | apply, use, produce, complete, calculate, draw                                           | Use the scenario details in the answer. Do not answer in general — tie back to the brief. |
| AO3 | **Analyse** information, causes, consequences, options                   | analyse, compare, explain the impact, why, examine                                       | Break the issue down. Show cause → effect. Link factors together.                         |
| AO4 | **Evaluate** and make justified judgements / recommendations             | evaluate, justify, recommend, assess, to what extent, discuss                            | Weigh pros vs cons, use the scenario evidence, give a clear conclusion with reasoning.    |

### Distinction-level checklist (use for every long-answer question)

- [ ] Uses correct technical vocabulary (AO1)
- [ ] Refers directly to the scenario / brief details (AO2)
- [ ] Explains cause and effect / trade-offs (AO3)
- [ ] Reaches a **justified judgement or recommendation** (AO4)
- [ ] Considers stakeholders, risk, cost, time, quality
- [ ] Concludes clearly — no fence-sitting
